Privacy Policy

Application Name: KaamPlus

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application KaamPlus ("Application") for mobile devices that was created by .haris & Noor.. The Application is an all-in-one productivity tool designed to offer Notes with full notebook view, Daily Tasks management, Expense Tracking, a built-in Document Scanner, Calculator, Age Calculator, and Zakat Calculator.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

The Application is designed with a strong focus on user privacy. Almost all information and data processed by the Application is stored locally on your device.

a. Personal Data

We do not collect, harvest, or request any personally identifiable information (PII) like your name, email address, physical address, or phone number. All data, including notes, tasks, expenses, and calculation histories, is kept local to your device.

b. Device Permissions and Hardware Features

To provide full functionality, the Application requests the following permissions:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Necessary for the Document Scanner feature to capture images of cards (like NIC) or letters for processing and automatic 4-corner cropping. No captured images are uploaded to any external server; they are processed entirely on-device.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used for tactile/haptic feedback when interacting with UI buttons (e.g., calculator keys).
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to initialize and load Google AdMob test advertisements.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send local notifications and reminders for your saved tasks and events.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertisements)

This Application integrates Google AdMob SDK to display advertisements. To help AdMob deliver relevant or non-personalized test advertisements, Google may collect and use certain device information (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and device model). 3. Data Retention and Security

We are committed to securing your data. Since your notes, scanned documents, and lists are stored locally in the Application's private storage (SQLite/Room Database), deleting the Application or clearing its cache will permanently remove your stored content. We recommend using device backups to protect your local data.

4. Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our records.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
